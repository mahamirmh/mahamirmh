SELECTED CASE STUDY / EDUCATION / MULTI-TENANT PLATFORM

Hamyar
School

Designing a coherent operating system for school communities.

Multi-tenancy · Role-based UX · Data quality · Release engineering
01 / THE CHALLENGE

A real operational problem.

School operations span multiple user groups — administrators, educators, students and families — each with different permissions, workflows and data needs. A usable platform must keep those experiences connected without compromising tenant boundaries.

02 / APPROACH & SCOPE

From ambiguity to structure.

  • School-specific tenant experience and theme architecture
  • Role-specific interface patterns for school staff, teachers, students and parents
  • Data normalization and school records import workflows
  • Release verification, staging, health checks and production readiness practices
03 / SYSTEM THINKING

Architecture as a product decision.

Multi-tenant runtime, scoped user roles, PostgreSQL/Prisma migrations and a role-aware frontend experience.

04 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Where I contributed.

Product direction, UI/UX systems, engineering coordination and delivery oversight across the school platform work.

05 / DOCUMENTED PROGRESS

Evidence over inflated metrics.

Documented implementation work includes school-specific tenant configuration, interface modernization, PostgreSQL migration tracking and deployment health verification.

Confidentiality: Student and school records are sensitive; this public case study intentionally omits source datasets, identifiable student information and private dashboards.

Quantitative business outcomes have not been independently verified and are intentionally not claimed.

WHAT MATTERS

Technology should make a real process easier to run.

Product thinking, engineering discipline and responsible delivery — brought together.
